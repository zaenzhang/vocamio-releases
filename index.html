VocaMio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

VocaMio is an offline Hungarian dictionary provided by Zaen Zhang. This policy applies to the VocaMio Android offline edition, application ID io.vocamio.app.

Privacy and support contact: zaenzhang@outlook.com.

Dictionary use stays on your device

You can search the bundled dictionary, view definitions, save words, and use the built-in Hungarian pronunciation without an account or an internet connection. We do not operate an account system, dictionary query server, cloud sync service, advertising service, or analytics service for this edition. The app does not send your searches, saved words, or reading history to the developer.

Text you type, share with VocaMio, or select through Android's text actions is processed on your device to find dictionary entries. We do not upload that text. The app does not read your clipboard in the background.

Information stored locally

VocaMio stores the identifiers of saved words, your most recent 50 different dictionary entries, and your interface language preference in Android app-private storage. The built-in speech engine also copies its bundled language resources into app-private storage. This edition has no user account, online profile, or developer-operated backup of these records.

Saved words remain until you remove them. Older history entries are replaced when the 50-entry limit is reached. You can clear history from My, remove saved words using their bookmark controls, or delete all local app data using Android Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Android automatic app backup is disabled for this edition. We cannot restore records that you delete or lose with your device.

Pronunciation and device access

The default built-in eSpeak NG engine synthesizes the selected word or example on your device and plays audio through Android. VocaMio does not record your voice or access your microphone, camera, contacts, or precise location for dictionary use.

You may choose an installed system text-to-speech engine. VocaMio lists Hungarian voices that the engine reports as installed and not requiring a network connection, then passes the selected dictionary text to that engine on your device. The chosen engine is supplied by its own provider and operates under that provider's privacy policy. Voice installation or the provider's settings may involve separate downloads or network use.

Links and support messages

Dictionary attribution links open your browser only when you choose them. The browser and destination website receive the URL you open, which can include the dictionary word, and handle the visit under their own privacy policies. Visiting a website is separate from an offline dictionary lookup.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use these messages to respond to support, correction, or privacy requests, and retain them only as needed to resolve the matter and meet applicable legal obligations. Email is handled through the email providers used by you and us. Avoid including information that is unnecessary for your request. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Collection, sharing, and security

The app does not transmit user data to the developer, sell user data, or use advertising identifiers, tracking cookies, analytics, or marketing profiles. Local processing and storage are used to provide the dictionary features described above. User-initiated browser visits, support email, and your selected system speech engine have the separate boundaries described in this policy.

Local records rely on Android's app storage protections and your device security. We do not claim that the app adds a separate encrypted vault or that support email is end-to-end encrypted. Google Play, Android, your browser, keyboard, and other services you choose operate under their own policies; this policy describes VocaMio's behavior.

Your privacy requests

Where applicable data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, or to request a portable copy, you can contact us using the email above. We use voluntary support correspondence to answer your request and for our legitimate interest in providing support; we also comply with applicable legal obligations. You may raise a concern with your local data protection authority. We cannot access or delete records that exist only in your device's app storage; use the local controls described above for those records.

Children and changes

VocaMio is a general language reference app. It does not create child profiles or target users with advertising. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can address the request.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the revised date here. For privacy questions, contact Zaen Zhang at zaenzhang@outlook.com.

VocaMio 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 27 日

VocaMio 是由 Zaen Zhang 提供的离线匈牙利语词典。本政策适用于 VocaMio Android 离线版，应用 ID 为 io.vocamio.app。

隐私与支持联系邮箱：zaenzhang@outlook.com。

查词在你的设备上完成

你无需注册或联网，即可查询内置词库、阅读释义、收藏词条并使用内置匈牙利语发音。本版本不提供账户、在线查询服务器、云同步、广告或分析统计服务。应用不会将查询、收藏和查阅历史发送给开发者。

你输入、分享给 VocaMio，或通过 Android 选词操作发送的文字，仅在设备上用于查找词条，不会被上传。应用不会在后台读取剪贴板。

本机保存的信息

VocaMio 在 Android 应用私有存储中保存收藏词条的标识、最近查阅的 50 个不同词条，以及界面语言偏好。内置语音引擎还会将随包语言资源复制到应用私有存储。本版本没有用户账户、在线个人资料或由开发者保存的记录备份。

收藏会保留到你主动移除；历史超过 50 个词条后会替换较早的记录。你可以在“我的”中清空历史，通过词条收藏按钮移除收藏，或在 Android 设置中清除全部应用数据。卸载也会删除本机数据。本版本禁用了 Android 自动应用备份。开发者无法恢复你已删除或随设备遗失的记录。

发音与设备访问

默认内置的 eSpeak NG 引擎在设备上合成所选词条或例句，并通过 Android 播放声音。查词过程中，VocaMio 不录制你的声音，也不访问麦克风、相机、联系人或精确位置。

你可以选择已安装的系统文字转语音引擎。VocaMio 会列出引擎报告为已安装且无需联网的匈牙利语声音，并将所选词典文字交给该设备上的引擎。系统引擎由其提供方维护，适用提供方的隐私政策；声音安装或提供方的设置可能需要另外下载或联网。

外部链接与支持邮件

只有你主动打开词库来源链接时，应用才会调用浏览器。浏览器和目标网站会接收你打开的网址；该网址可能包含所查词条。浏览器和网站按照各自的隐私政策处理访问，这与离线查词是不同的操作。

如果你发邮件给我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你主动提供的内容，用于回复支持、词条纠错或隐私请求。邮件仅保留到解决问题及履行适用法律义务所需的期限，由你我使用的邮件服务提供方处理。请不要发送与请求无关的信息。你可以通过联系邮箱请求查阅、更正或删除支持邮件中的个人信息；适用法律另有要求的除外。

数据收集、共享与安全

应用不会向开发者传输用户数据，不出售用户数据，也不使用广告标识符、追踪 Cookie、分析统计或营销画像。本机处理和存储仅用于提供上述词典功能。由你发起的浏览器访问、支持邮件和可选系统语音引擎，分别遵循本政策说明的边界。

本机记录依赖 Android 的应用存储保护和你的设备安全。我们不声称应用提供了额外的加密保险箱，也不声称支持邮件经过端到端加密。Google Play、Android、浏览器、输入法及你选择使用的其他服务适用各自的政策；本政策说明的是 VocaMio 本身的行为。

你的隐私请求

如果适用的数据保护法律赋予你查阅、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理或获取可携带副本的权利，可以通过上述邮箱联系我们。我们处理你主动发送的支持邮件，是为了回应你的请求及提供支持这一合法利益，并履行适用的法律义务。你也可以向所在地的数据保护主管机关反映问题。仅保存在设备应用存储中的记录无法由开发者远程访问或删除，请使用上文说明的本机操作处理。

儿童与政策更新

VocaMio 是一般语言参考应用，不创建儿童档案，也不针对用户投放广告。如果你认为儿童通过支持邮件向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策并修改上方日期。如有隐私问题，请联系 Zaen Zhang：zaenzhang@outlook.com。
